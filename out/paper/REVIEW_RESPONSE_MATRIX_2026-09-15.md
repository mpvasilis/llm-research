## Evidence still required for stronger claims

   targets. The current count is 0/120 independently human-labeled audit
   sentences.
2. Recover or regenerate stagewise raw responses before extending detector
   component or output-window conclusions beyond final 7B.
3. Run a checkpoint-by-serialization crossover before interpreting Base-to-SFT
   as a training effect.
4. Keep the completed exploratory DPO conditional odds ratios distinct from
   probability effects and aggregate absolute differences; do not describe the
   model as removing length bias or identifying causal effects.
5. Obtain an independent readability check from readers unfamiliar with the
   project.
